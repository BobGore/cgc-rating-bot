# Privacy Policy — CGC Rating Bot

Last updated: 16 September 2026

## What the bot stores

When you add yourself to the rating list, the bot stores:

- your chess site username
- the chess site
- the time control
- your Discord user ID

Nothing else is kept. The bot does not store message content.

## How it is used

- The data is used only to show the rating list on the server.
- To show ratings, the bot sends your chess username to that chess site's public API. Ratings are already public on those sites.

## Where it is kept

- The data is held only on computers controlled by the maintainer.
- It is not published, shared, or sold, and it is not stored in the GitHub repository.

## Removing your data

- Type `!remove` in the rating channel to delete your own entry. Only you or the maintainer can remove it.
- Alternatively, send the maintainer a message in the rating channel asking to be removed.

## Changes

This policy may be updated. The current version is always the one in this repository.
